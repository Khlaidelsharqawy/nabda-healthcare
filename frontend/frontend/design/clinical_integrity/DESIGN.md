## Brand & Style
This design system embodies medical precision, privacy, high human empathy, and clinical trustworthiness. It deliberately rejects consumer tech tropes and speculative artificial intelligence aesthetics—forbidding neon orbs, vibrating violet gradients, floating sparkles, and decorative chat embellishments. 

Instead, artificial intelligence is treated strictly as an accountable medical instrument: deterministic, verified, bounded, and transparent. Every computational insight is explicitly contextualized, labeled with its operational boundary, and anchored by explicit human-in-the-loop review states.

The visual style blends modern corporate architecture with clinical clarity:
- **Calm, High-Information Surfaces:** Clear hierarchy optimized for cognitive offloading during critical healthcare decisions.
- **Architectural Subtlety:** Structure is achieved through micro-borders, deliberate tonal separation, and restrained depth rather than optical illusions or hyper-stylized ornamentation.
- **Multi-Role Adaptability:** Seamlessly transitions between dense clinical documentation workspaces (for physicians), operational triage lanes (for medical assistants), aggregate analytics (for healthcare administrators), and reassuring, calm portals (for patients).
- **Native Bi-Directional Harmony:** Full structural parity between Left-to-Right (English) and Right-to-Left (Egyptian Arabic) medical terminology, maintaining rhythm, optical weight, and layout density across both scripts.

## Colors
The palette balances institutional clinical stability with absolute clarity in life-critical decision paths. Color is applied with semantic discipline; functional hues are never repurposed as mere visual decoration.

### Primary Medical Brand
- **Primary Emerald (`#087443`):** The operational core. Conveys validation, clinical authority, and safe action.
- **Forest (`#054F2E`):** High-contrast interactive states, primary focus rings, and high-priority confirmed items.
- **Sage Light (`#ECFDF3`):** Positive status backgrounds, accepted clinical findings, and verified AI triage containers.
- **Mint Border (`#A6F4C5`):** Structural boundaries for verified medical records and stable patient parameters.

### Secondary & Functional Indicators
- **Indigo/Steel (`#3538CD` / Tint `#EEF4FF`):** Deterministic computational processing, clinical telemetry, voice session streaming states, and data provenance.
- **Warm Gold / Amber (`#B54708` / Tint `#FEF0C7`):** Unverified clinical suggestions, human review bottlenecks, drug allergy caveats, and dosage friction warnings.
- **Muted Rose (`#D92D20` / Tint `#FEE4E2`):** Contraindications, critical vital alerts, triage escalation flags, and structural errors.

### Neutral Foundation
- **Charcoal (`#181D27`):** Primary display headers, dominant medical metrics, and core narrative body copy.
- **Graphite (`#2A313C`):** Form labels, secondary clinical data values, and active tab states.
- **Stone (`#535862`):** Metadata, audit log timestamps, unit labels, and inactive iconography.
- **Light Gray (`#E9EAEB`):** Ghost borders, tabular rules, and neutral dividers.
- **Off-white (`#F8F9FA`):** Canvas background, secondary panel fills, and inset clinical cards.
- **Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`):** Primary card elevations, modal sheets, and active field surfaces.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy prioritizes rapid scannability, structural clarity, and zero ambiguity in reading numerical dosages, laboratory ranges, and pharmacological nomenclature.

### Font Family Pairing
- **Latin Display & Headings:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides contemporary clinical authority with subtle geometry that avoids cold clinical sterility.
- **Latin Body, Data, and Controls:** Inter ensures maximum legibility under high-density table views and small-screen diagnostics.
- **Arabic Script Architecture (عافية):** For all Arabic localization, the system shifts strictly to **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** for interface controls, body copy, and headlines. It maintains the identical typographic baseline and optical sizing scale, ensuring effortless comprehension of Egyptian medical dialect and standard formal Arabic without layout displacement.

### Implementation Rules
- **Clinical Data (`clinical-data`):** Used strictly for diagnostic badges, blood pressure values, lab indicators, and multi-tenant instance status pills. Must always render with tabular numbers (`tnum`) enabled in CSS.
- **Micro-Copy (`micro-copy`):** Reserved for verification timestamps, doctor digital signatures, AI inference confidence tags, and tenant isolation identifiers.
- **Bilingual Line Heights:** When localized into Arabic, all vertical line-height rules automatically scale up by an additional factor of 1.15 to account for taller Arabic ascenders and descenders without clipping glyphs.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system is built on a multi-tier responsive grid engineered to handle disparate operational scenarios: high-density multi-column charts for clinical encounters, sequential triage boards for assistants, and high-accessibility views for patients.

### Grid Framework & Form Factors
- **Desktop Clinical Workspace (1440px+):** 12-column fluid grid. Gutters: `24px` (`gutter`). Margins: `32px` (`margin`). Allows a persistent 3-column split: Left (Patient Baseline & Chronic History), Center (Active Consultation & AI Voice Live Transcription), Right (Structured Order Entry & Drug Interactions).
- **Tablet Clinical Triage (768px – 1439px):** 8-column layout. Gutters: `16px` (`gutter-sm`). Margins: `24px`. Side panels collapse into off-canvas sliding sheets.
- **Mobile Handheld (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout. Gutters: `16px`. Margins: `16px` (`margin-sm`). Stacks all operational data into vertical clinical cards with bottom-anchored actions.

### Spacing Philosophy
- **Density Scaling:** The system provides two operational density variants:
  - *Standard Mode (Public, Patient Portal, Admin):* Employs standard rhythm using `space-md` (`16px`) and `space-lg` (`24px`) for calm white space.
  - *Compact Clinical Mode (Doctor EMR, Bedside Triage):* Reduces component padding to `space-sm` (`8px`) and internal gaps to `space-xs` (`4px`), permitting vital telemetry to remain visible above the fold without scrolling.
- **Directional Mirroring:** Margin and padding logic must rely on logical properties (`margin-inline-start`, `padding-inline-end`) to guarantee flawless RTL transitions in Arabic mode.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system avoids high-blur, dramatic drop shadows that create visual haze in high-density clinical environments. Instead, depth is structured through calibrated tonal layers and micro-borders, supplemented by minimal ambient shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8F9FA` (Dark mode: `#0F1319`). The structural canvas on which all work rests. Flat, no depth.
- **Level 1 (Clinical Work Panels & Containers):** `#FFFFFF` (Dark mode: `#161B22`). Outlined with a 1px crisp border in `#E9EAEB` (Dark mode: `#303746`). No shadow required; visual hierarchy is achieved purely via contrast against the Level 0 canvas.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards, Context Panels, Voice Transcripts):** `#FFFFFF` (Dark mode: `#212631`). Outlined with 1px `#E9EAEB`, accompanied by an ambient, low-spread shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.06), 0px 1px 2px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Dropdowns, Clinical Context Flyouts, Popovers):** Elevated contextual elements. Shadow: `0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.08), 0px 2px 4px -2px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.04)`. Border: 1px `#E9EAEB`.
- **Level 4 (Life-Critical Alerts, Overlays, Diagnostic Sheets):** Focused floating modals. Shadow: `0px 12px 16px -4px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.1), 0px 4px 6px -2px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.05)`. Modal backdrop utilizes a 40% opacity Charcoal fill (`#181D27`) with a 2px hardware-accelerated background blur.

### AI Depth Paradigm
AI-generated cards, suggestions, and context scopes do not use ethereal glows or shimmering depth. They utilize crisp, high-contrast tinted fills (Sage `#ECFDF3` for confirmed, Amber `#FEF0C7` for unreviewed, Indigo `#EEF4FF` for raw streaming) bordered with 1px saturated lines to indicate that computational processing has taken place.

## Shapes
A conservative, structural shape language reinforces medical authority, precision, and reliable utility. 

- **Corner Radius Scale:**
  - Base Elements (`0.25rem` / `4px`): Micro-badges, input fields, internal table rows, clinical data tags, and contextual pills.
  - Cards & Panels (`0.5rem` / `8px`): Patient encounter cards, diagnostic summaries, AI session transcription modules, and modal containers.
  - Structural Dialogs & Slide-overs (`0.75rem` / `12px`): Sheet dialogs and patient chart master modals.
- **Zero-Pill Doctrine:** Action buttons, input containers, and large clinical surfaces must never be fully pill-shaped (`9999px`), as round shapes erode the clinical, data-dense look and reduce usable tap area for technical medical terms.
- **Exception for Status Pills:** Full rounding is permitted exclusively on miniature status indicators and human-in-the-loop review tags (e.g., maximum height 20px) where an explicit visual distinction from actionable buttons is clinically vital.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Clinical Action:** Background `#087443`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, border none, radius `4px`. Hover state: `#054F2E`. Focus state: 2px offset ring in `#087443`.
- **Destructive/Emergency Action:** Background `#D92D20`, foreground `#FFFFFF`. Used for patient discharge overrides, critical allergy warnings, or record revocations.
- **Secondary / Ghost Action:** Crisp white surface with a 1px `#E9EAEB` border, text `#2A313C`. Active hover shifts to `#F8F9FA`.
- **State Feedback:** Buttons must never rely purely on color transitions. Focus, active, and disabled states feature high-contrast visible state boundaries. Disabled states must clearly specify the blocking condition via tooltip (e.g., "Doctor signature required").

### Scoped AI & Context Badges
- **Context Header Pill:** Displays operational scope: `Doctor AI Context Active`, `Patient Scope`, or `Assistant Logistics AI`. Constructed using an Indigo tint surface (`#EEF4FF`), 1px solid border (`#C7D7FE`), and crisp `#3538CD` text accompanied by a monospaced lock glyph.
- **Structured Suggestion Pills:** Computational diagnostic proposals appear in a warm amber or sage tone (e.g., "Suggested Diagnosis: Acute Sinusitis"). Contains an icon indicator, the proposed term, a confidence index (e.g., "94% correlation"), and two direct actions: an accept tick (`✓`) and a reject cross (`✕`).
- **Human Review Indicators:** Displayed prominently next to every algorithmic deduction: a dual-state badge stating either "Pending Human Attestation" (Amber) or "Physician Verified" (Emerald with physician timestamp).

### Clinical Voice Session Cards
- Structured transcription cards that record doctor-patient dialogue in real time.
- Bordered with an Indigo accent border on the inline-start edge (`3px solid #3538CD`).
- Interior panel contains a dual split: the live audio stream waveform and real-time normalized clinical text, paired with immediate medical entity extraction pills (medications, symptoms, dosages) displayed in an interactive sidebar.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Default State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E9EAEB`, text `#181D27`, radius `4px`. Height: `36px` in Compact Mode, `44px` in Standard.
- **Validation States:** Critical errors (e.g., invalid dosage quantity) render in 1.5px `#D92D20` with helper text below in matching hue. Never rely on color alone; each error displays an inline alert icon.
- **Bilingual Bidirectionality:** Placeholder text, numeric values, and trailing measurement units (e.g., `mg/dL`, `mmHg`) automatically flip optical order based on the active locale, ensuring dosage units remain bound correctly to the number.

### Lists, Tables & Medical Record Grids
- **Header Structure:** Background `#F8F9FA`, uppercase text in `11px`, font weight `600`, text `#535862`.
- **Row Styling:** Subtle divider lines (`1px solid #E9EAEB`), minimum height `44px` (clinical dense mode). Row hover state shifts subtly to `#F8F9FA`.
- **Tabular Numerics:** All clinical values (vitals, lab metrics, insurance IDs) must enforce monospace/tabular numerical formatting to maintain vertical decimal alignment.

### Cards & Patient Envelopes
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E9EAEB`, radius `8px`.
- High-priority cards (e.g., Active Resuscitation, Severe Interaction Flag) utilize a top border highlight (3px solid `#D92D20`).
- Header actions, context tags, and timestamp indicators are positioned along the header margin to prevent clinical notes from breaking prematurely.
