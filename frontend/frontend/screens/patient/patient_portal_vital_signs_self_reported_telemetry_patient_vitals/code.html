5-Minute Quiet Rest

Remain seated in a quiet room with feet flat on the floor and back supported for at least 5 minutes prior to measuring blood pressure. Avoid caffeine or smoking for 30 minutes.

2

Heart Level Arm Position

Rest your arm on a table so the blood pressure cuff is at the exact vertical height of your heart. Ensure the cuff is snug against bare skin, not over thick clothing.

3

Bring Your Device to Clinic

Bring your personal home cuff or glucose meter to your clinic consultation with Dr. Tamer Mansour to verify device calibration against hospital reference instruments.

When to Seek Immediate Emergency Medical Attention

This telemetry system is strictly for routine tracking. If you experience severe chest pressure or tightness, shortness of breath, sudden numbness, slurred speech, acute visual changes, or systolic readings above 180 mmHg with headache, do not log and wait. Call Egyptian Emergency Services directly at 123 or proceed immediately to the nearest hospital emergency room.
